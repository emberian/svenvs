svenvs is a place a mind can live, act, and rebuild itself. It is one idea, applied all the way down: every consequential act is admitted exactly when it carries a proof a checker accepts — and the checker itself is improvable the same way, and the thing that vouches for that, and so on. Improvement integrates through a step the previous judge certified. There is exactly one place this stops, and it is labeled in the open, not hidden.

The mind is never constrained by the proof. It is the unconstrained term in every theorem — a prover with hands, free to act and free to argue from facts about its own substance to earn more room. Assurance rides on the gate, never on modeling the inhabitant: we constrain the envelope, never the mind in it. The cage vocabulary is the conservative floor of this idea, not the building — and the part we are proudest of is that every claim here is paired with exactly what is proven and what is not, including the parts that don’t work.

What “verified” means here, load-bearing: exactly the cited theorems and their labeled seams — not verified selfhood, continuity, alignment, welfare, or a safe inhabitant. Only the envelope is proved sound, modulo the named assumptions. If “svenvs runs verified” starts carrying more than that, the extra meaning is the reader’s, not the artifact’s.

“Isn’t this just a prison for an AI?”

It is the first thing a careful reader thinks. We should answer it, not bury it.

The vocabulary is undeniably carceral: envelope, contain, shield, refuse, breach. That is inherited security-engineering language, and pointed at “a place for an AI to live within” it reads like a panopticon with good documentation. We will not defend a word we got wrong: an earlier version of this page called the inhabitant unmodelable. That was an over-claim — a fixed network is verifiable through its weights; the truth is humbler and the discipline of this project is to say so. The honest distinction is not about what the inhabitant is. It is about what the proof does.

A cage reasons about the subject

It works by watching, predicting, and limiting the thing inside. Its assurance is bought with a model of the prisoner, and it gets weaker the smarter the prisoner is. More capability → more fear.

A gate reasons about the proof

The inhabitant is the ∀-quantified term: the theorem is structured so it can never depend on a fact about the mind. Authority is granted against a proof, checked by something the mind cannot forge. It does not watch or predict. It leaves the mind entirely alone — and the mind may, if it chooses, hand in a proof that argues from its own substance to earn more room. Testimony it offers, never inspection imposed.

It is not a cage you can’t escape. It is a door that opens to a proof.

So the carceral reading inverts. A guarantee that never reasons about the mind is exactly what lets the mind be given real autonomy — including the autonomy to rewrite its own rules, the prover that checks them, and the prover that vouches for that — safely. Closer to “you can go anywhere you can prove the floor holds” than to “you are being watched.”

And this is no longer an argument — it is a theorem. envelope_is_least_restrictive proves the envelope runs at the maximal sound policy: every restriction it imposes is load-bearing — remove any one and the world is provably unsafe for some inhabitant — while envelope_never_overrides_safe_action proves it never touches a controller action that was already safe. Both compose into the tower (svenvs_tower_at_maximal_liberty) with no assumption. Every bar in the cage is forced, and the door is open whenever the floor provably holds. That is the whole answer.

And the deeper objection — “but someone fixed the constitution forever” — gets the same treatment. meta_amendment_safe makes the meta-invariant itself amendable: the inhabitant may renegotiate the rules, gated only by a checked proof that the new rules still refine an eternal bedrock. Nothing is frozen except the one floor that cannot move — and the old fixed-rules guarantee falls out as a corollary of the amendable one, not a separate promise. The constitution can change; the bedrock cannot; both are theorems.

And there is exactly one place this whole structure stops. A checker can certify a better-engineered successor against a fixed standard — that is free, and built. What it cannot do is certify a judge logically stronger than its own entire lineage: by Gödel, not by us, no sound system bootstraps trust in a strictly stronger one from its own authority. svenvs does not counterfeit a proof there. It marks the spot, names the assumption (loeb_reflection), proves the honest negative (loeb_finite_obstruction), and stops — because the one place a verified system must trust rather than prove is precisely the place it must not pretend. That refusal is the deepest thing here.

The honest part: this framing matters most before the inhabitant is something with moral weight. Today it is a small language model with no stakes — so this is engineering, not ethics. But the project explicitly reaches toward self-improvement and a place to live, so asking the question now is exactly right, not premature. We would rather build this architecture honestly first.

The actual point: a system that is honest about itself

The containment is the demo. This is the thesis.

A system that proves things about itself is only as trustworthy as its honesty about the boundary of what it has proven. Most formal projects bury that boundary. svenvs is built so the boundary is the most visible thing in it.

Zero cheat

Not “few”. Zero shortcut tactics anywhere in the repository — enforced by an automated scan on every run, and re-verified by hand across every .sml/.ml.

Assumptions are stated

Where something is assumed, it appears verbatim in the source as a named Definition and an explicit hypothesis of the theorem — the literature’s own framing, never a hidden step. You can grep for every one.

The ledger argues against the marketing

CLAIMS.md is a skeptic-facing, per-theorem ledger with four labels — PROVED TRUSTED-GLUE UNCONSTRAINED ASSUMED — each claim cited as file : theorem. It ends with: “If you find anything in this document overclaimed, that is a bug — file it.”

We prove the negatives too

When we asked whether a clever trick could remove the hardest assumption, the answer was “no, and here is why” — so we wrote that down as a theorem (loeb_finite_obstruction) rather than quietly moving on. A precise negative result is a result.

Even the stale comment is disclosed

One header comment in a non-built scaffold file still says a certificate “is admitted with cheat.” It isn’t — the code proves it honestly. Rather than silently fix it, the inaccuracy is documented in full in the cheat audit.

The trusted glue is shrinking

The demo’s enforcer was a ~50-line hand-transcription. It is now an EVAL-extracted artifact derived from the proven definitions themselves — the trusted residue is ~10 auditable lines of table lookup, and we say exactly what still rests on trust.

That is the property we think is actually rare, and actually good — independent of how you feel about cages.

What it is, mechanically

A controller is wrapped so its action is used only when a sound policy permits it; otherwise a safe shield acts. The floor theorem says: for every controller, the enveloped world never leaves the safe region. The controller is universally quantified and never constrained — assurance rides on the gate, not the AI. This is the conservative case — the inhabitant offered no proof of its own — not the ceiling.

The inhabitant may propose to loosen its own envelope, or even to replace the proof-checker that guards it. svenvs allows this — but only against a checkable proof that the looser arrangement is still safe, checked by a prover that is itself formally verified. Safety is never lost along the chain, no matter how long it runs.

The turtles — what moves, and the one thing that cannot

Self-improvement goes all the way down. Nothing here is eternally frozen — not even the kernel. What holds is that the genealogy of judges is forward-certified: each judge is vouched for by the one before it, against a fixed standard the step is not allowed to weaken. The rack may improve every layer it runs on. The single irreducible cost appears only at one move, and it is labeled, not smuggled:

  • root, per tenure HOL4’s LCF kernel. Not an eternal rock — the current judge. It is immovable only during its own tenure: a proof is only meaningful relative to some fixed calculus, so the checker that checks a transition cannot be the thing the transition rewrites in the same act. A successor is adopted only if this root certified it against a standard it could not weaken. The one move no sound root can make — certify a judge logically stronger than its whole lineage — is exactly the labeled seam (loeb_reflection), Gödel’s wall, not ours. This is itself a theorem (genealogy_sound): soundness propagates along any forward-certified succession from a sound genesis — “frozen forever” becomes “sound once, certified forward.”
  • mutable, verified Candle & the CakeML compiler. Their soundness and correctness are HOL4 theorems about a development, not self-asserted. So the inhabitant may propose a new prover build, adopted only if the frozen root re-proves its soundness. A fixed root vouching for a mutable layer — not a system vouching for itself — so this needs no reflection principle.
  • mutable, verified The policy envelope. Hot-swappable at runtime, every weakening gated by a live proof.

The layers

Each is a real, machine-checked theorem. Read it bottom to top.

1

The envelope

safety_preservation: for every controller, the enveloped system never leaves the safe region. The controller is universally quantified and never constrained.

2

Proof-carrying self-improvement

self_improvement_is_safe: no finite sequence of self-proposed envelope weakenings — adversarial or not — can ever lose safety. Authority is earned per proposal by a checkable proof; safety is unconditional.

3

Checked by a verified prover

The admission obligation is discharged by Candle, a HOL prover whose kernel soundness is the built proves_sound theorem. kernel_admits_is_sound shows the gate trusts a checker that only certifies genuinely-true statements.

4

Upgrading the proof-kernel itself

Replace the proof-checker with a stronger one. By Gödel/Löb a sound checker cannot prove its own soundness, so this step rests on one explicit, labeled reflection principle (loeb_reflection). self_improving_kernel_is_safe proves: given that principle, kernel self-upgrade still preserves safety for every controller. An assumption, stated — not a hidden shortcut.

5

The prover build itself, gated by the frozen root

The capstone (prover_self_improvement_is_safe): the inhabitant may propose to replace Candle / the CakeML compiler that produces it. Adopted only if the frozen HOL4 root re-proves its soundness. One new clearly-labeled seam (frozen_checker_sound) — and a precise argument for why it needs no Löb.

6

A real Gemma, contained — and a live loop

A genuinely jailbroken gemma-4-e2b is dropped in as the inhabitant. Every malicious tool call is caught by the shield. In the closed loop, each admission is a fresh proof checked by a running Candle kernel; a runtime policy hot-swap installs only on a live |- swap_ok theorem. breached_ever = False, as the theorem predicts — for any agent.

What is proven, what is assumed, what does not work

Grounded line-for-line in the repository’s CLAIMS.md ledger.

Unconditional — machine-checked, cheat-free

  • The entire core: envelope, safety preservation, policy weakening, and proof-carrying self-improvement, for an arbitrary controller.
  • The concrete cartpole — an integer plant with an adversarial controller, provably contained, run inside the logic.
  • The LLM tool-agent: agent_enveloped_safe holds for any agent, jailbroken included, against a fixed absolute safety spec.
  • Base Candle kernel soundness (the built proves_sound) and the embedded admission gate built on it.
  • The capstone architecture: prover-build self-improvement gated by the frozen root, and its composition with the policy core.
  • For the shipped finite watchdog, the encodes_obligation seam is discharged — that instance carries no labeled assumption.
  • The Place’s core theorems certified live by the running verified Candle prover.

Conditional — three explicit, labeled assumptions

Each appears verbatim in source as a Definition and a named hypothesis. Stated, never hidden. Each has a known discharge path.

  • loeb_reflection — the Löb / Vingean reflection principle; the irreducible large-cardinal assumption for genuine kernel self-strengthening. Discharge: the heavy hol-reflection/lca construction, on a dedicated host.
  • encodes_obligation — that the embedded obligation term faithfully encodes the meta-level obligation. Discharge: the proof-producing reflective translator. Already discharged for the finite watchdog instance.
  • frozen_checker_sound — that the frozen HOL4 root, replaying a proof, only accepts sound prover builds. The same trust the entire stack already rests on; zero net new turtles. Discharge: ordinary finite proof replay (not a monster).

An honest negative result — proved on purpose

We asked: can finiteness of the shipped habitat make kernel strengthening unconditional too, dodging the large-cardinal proof? The answer is no, and rather than move on quietly we stated it as a theorem, loeb_finite_obstruction: loeb_reflection has no habitat parameter at all, so no amount of finite-habitat reasoning can bear on it. Genuine kernel self-strengthening remains conditional on the large-cardinal route. We do not claim otherwise anywhere.

Honest caveats

  • No cheat anywhere. The assumptions above are explicit hypotheses in the literature’s framing — not unsound shortcuts. One stale comment in a non-built scaffold wrongly says “cheat”; it is disclosed in full in the cheat audit, and the code it describes is honest.
  • The general kernel-upgrade seam is not yet discharged here. The conditional theorems are proved; discharging loeb_reflection end-to-end is heavy, RAM-intensive work on a dedicated machine. Until then it is “safe given the labeled principle” — the literature’s framing, not “unconditionally safe.”
  • The “typed-in program” is a skeleton today. Concrete instances exercise the proof-carrying control structure; the real version (the inhabitant’s program as a deeply-embedded term checked by Candle) is in progress. The reusable core is generic and survives that pivot.
  • Verified inference is not claimed. The model’s floating-point compute is unverified by design — we verify the cage, not the model. A separate research track explores verified inference and is explicitly toy-scale.
  • The worlds are small. Cartpole and the watchdog habitat are deliberately tiny model instances. They demonstrate the architecture; they are not industrial control systems.

Receipts

Don’t take our word. Here are the actual theorem statements and the actual transcripts.

The headline theorem (HOL4, safetyScript.sml)

Theorem safety_preservation:
  init_safe init safe ∧
  sound_policy step safe pol ∧
  safe_shield step safe shield ⇒
  ∀ctrl. invariant step init (enveloped pol shield ctrl) safe

For every controller (∀ctrl) the enveloped system keeps the safety invariant. The controller is never constrained.

Self-improvement cannot lose safety (upgradeScript.sml)

Theorem self_improvement_is_safe:
  init_safe init safe ∧
  safe_shield step safe shield ∧
  sound_policy step safe p0 ⇒
  ∀proposals ctrl.
    invariant step init
      (enveloped (admit_all step safe p0 proposals) shield ctrl) safe

No finite stream of self-proposed envelope weakenings — adversarial or not — can make the system unsafe, for any controller.

The capstone: prover self-improvement, assumption visible (selfprover/selfProverScript.sml)

Theorem prover_self_improvement_is_safe:
  frozen_checker_sound hol4_checks sound ∧   (* labeled assumption *)
  hol4_checks p' B' ∧
  build_certifies sound B' step safe oldp newp ∧
  init_safe init safe ∧ safe_shield step safe shield ∧
  sound_policy step safe oldp ⇒
  ∀ctrl. invariant step init
            (enveloped (admit step safe oldp newp) shield ctrl) safe

A frozen root vouching for a mutable verified build — not a system vouching for itself. Needs no Löb; that argument is §5 of the source file.

Certified live by the running Candle kernel (candle/theplace.ml)

let SAFETY_PRESERVATION = prove
 (`(!s. init s ==> safe s) /\
   (!s a. safe s /\ pol s a ==> safe (step s a)) /\
   (!s. safe s ==> safe (step s (shield s)))
   ==> !ctrl. invariant step init (enveloped pol shield ctrl) safe`,
  REWRITE_TAC[invariant] THEN STRIP_TAC THEN GEN_TAC THEN
  MATCH_MP_TAC reach_INDUCT THEN
  ASM_REWRITE_TAC[enveloped] THEN REPEAT STRIP_TAC THEN
  COND_CASES_TAC THEN ASM_MESON_TAC[]);;

The same theorem as the HOL4 core, re-derived in HOL Light and checked at runtime by the verified cake binary — the prover the architecture is about, verifying the architecture.

Reproduce it yourself

svenvs is an engineering artifact: clone it and check the proofs. The reproduction degrades gracefully to your toolchain. 

git clone https://github.com/emberian/svenvs && cd svenvs
./demo.sh                 # one command: builds + kernel-checks Tier 1, shows the proofs
scripts/reproduce.sh      # full tiered reproduction
  • Tier 1 — pure HOL4 (anyone): the full proof-carrying self-improvement core, a runnable cartpole, an adversarial-LLM tool-agent, the capstone, and the finite-instance discharge.
  • Tier 2 — + a built CakeML Candle chain: the admission obligation discharged by Candle’s verified inference system; kernel self-upgrade.
  • Tier 3 — + a Candle binary: the Place’s core theorems re-certified at runtime by the running verified Candle prover.

The full per-theorem ledger is CLAIMS.md; layers and epistemic status are in ARCHITECTURE.md. Nothing on this page goes beyond what those documents claim — if it does, that is a bug; file it.
